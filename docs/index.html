Read-only · single binary · no runtime

A document viewer that opens before you blink.

Small, portable, cross-platform, summoned by a keystroke. No editing, no annotation, no cloud, no plugins — one window, one document, nothing else.

Register with the desktop

# Look at it without a display
feather-viewer --screenshot out.png document.pdf

# Linux — a launcher and a MIME candidate
packaging/linux/install.sh

# Windows — HKCU only, no admin rights, no installer
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File packaging\windows\install.ps1

Both register Feather as a candidate for the formats it can open, and neither seizes the default. Choosing that is your job, in your own settings, where you can also undo it.

Not in it

Tabs, pins and your reading position are restored between runs, and the file lives where your platform expects.

Editing, annotation, printing, cloud, plugins, telemetry, an updater, a settings window, Office documents, a tray icon (it needs GTK on Linux — the right answer is a D-Bus status item, which is its own piece of work).
